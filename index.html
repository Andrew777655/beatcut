    Auto-transcribe from the song

    Runs a speech model in this tab — your audio never leaves the machine, but the model itself downloads once from a CDN. Whisper is trained on speech, so sung vocals still come out rough even at best. Treat it as a draft to fix up, not a finished caption track. Bigger model = better words but a longer download and a slower run. Only the trimmed part of the song is transcribed, so shortening the edit first makes this much faster.
